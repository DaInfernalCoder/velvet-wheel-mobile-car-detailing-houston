# Velvet Wheel design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who notice the feel of a clean cabin and the finish of wheels and paint. The page's single job is to move a driver toward calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Velvet night — `#24152B`
- Plum metal — `#4A2D52`
- Silver cloth — `#E7E1E9`
- Pearl — `#FAF8FB`
- Cobalt rinse — `#4D8DFF`
- Brass brush — `#D4A84F`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

Reuse the editorial/service-process frame, but treat the hero curve as a close wheel arc and alternate soft cabin surfaces with crisp inspection bands.

## Signature

A single oversized wheel arc crosses the hero and returns as a circular inspection cue around the service sequence.

## Critique

The first idea risked looking like a generic luxury-detailing page. The revision avoids black-and-gold excess by using plum, cobalt rinse, and tactile silver fields; brass appears only on primary actions and inspection points.
